# Code 19 — Leave Policy (Human Resources, Edition No. 1.1, 15 January 2022)

## A. Definition & Scope
Leave means when an employee remains absent from duty having the consent/approval of the appropriate authority.
For the purpose of leave calculation for an employee, leave calendar will be from January 01 to December 31 of Standard English Year.
This policy is applicable for all permanent employees at Code 19, i.e. it does not apply to interns and contractual employees.

## B. Objective Of Leave Policy
Enabling an employee to meet personal or social needs. Motivating an employee to improve her/his performance. Allowing employee to recover from physical and/or psychological fatigue. Ensuring services and support functions are uninterrupted at Code 19.

## C. Leave Category
a) Casual Leave (CL) 10 Days
b) Sick Leave (SL) 14 Days
c) Earned Leave (EL) 20 Days
d) Paternal Leave (PL) 7 Days
e) Maternity Leave (ML) 120 Days
f) Leave Without Pay (LWP) As required

## D. Casual Leave (CL)
(1) The employee needs to inform her/his line manager about availing such leave through email prior to going on leave. In case of a sudden requirement or an emergency, the employee should notify the line manager over phone or message and take a post-dated approval over email.
(2) An employee can take leave for a period of maximum two (2) days at a stretch.
(3) Employee can also take half (1/2) day casual leave and the duration should not be more than four (4) hours.
(4) An employee will be entitled for this type of leave on pro-rata basis from the date of joining. Unspent leave balance will be foregone on December 31 of each year.

## E. Sick Leave (SL)
(1) An employee should take a post-dated email approval from line manager for leave and apply through leave form after recovering from sickness, if they were not in state to inform beforehand.
(2) The employee needs to submit medical certificate with the leave application form if the leave period is more than one (1) day.
(3) An employee will be entitled for this type of leave on pro-rata basis from the date of joining. Unspent leave balance will be foregone on December 31 of each year.

## F. Earned Leave (EL) / Annual Leave
(1) The employee should take email approval from line manager and submit leave application form at least ten (10) working days before availing such leave.
(2) Unspent earned leave balance can be carried over to proceeding year and maximum accumulation at any point of service will be sixty (60) days.
(3) An employee will be entitled for this type of leave on pro-rata basis from the date of confirmation.
(4) An employee can avail earned leave encashment at the time of separation only, i.e. resignation, termination, removal, discharge on medical ground and death. This benefit is not applicable to an employee when s/he is dismissed due to any gross misconduct.
(5) An employee cannot take earned leave of more than five (5) days consecutively at a particular point in time without approval from management, in addition to his/her line manager.

## G. Maternity Leave (ML)
(1) A female employee will apply for Maternity Leave at least ten (10) weeks in advance from expected date of availing such leave, supported with medical certificate from the attending physician.
(2) The employee should be allowed take this leave with full pay, assuming that they have followed the correct procedure of availing this leave.
(3) Maternity leave may be allowed to an employee for up to two (2) children during her tenure with the company. In case of delivering a third child birth, concerned employee may be able to avail other leaves except maternity leave or its associated benefit(s).

## H. Paternal Leave (PL)
(1) A male employee will apply for leave at least four (4) weeks in advance from expected date of availing such leave, supported with medical certificate of his partner from the attending physician.
(2) This leave is also applicable for two (2) children during his tenure and should be taken within six (6) weeks from his child's date of birth.

## I. Leave Without Pay (LWP)
(1) The employee may apply for leave when there is no leave balance available in her/his leave account or requires leave that exceeds her/his leave balance.
(2) Considering all circumstances, the Management may approve this leave subject to limiting her/him from annual increment, salary enhancement and any other admissible benefit(s) entitlement.
(3) An employee may take leave without pay in combination of other available leave balance, having prior approval from line manager.

## J. Unauthorized Absences
Any employee absent from work for consecutive 3 (three) working days at the first instance without an acceptable reason and without prior permission will lose pay for day/days of absence without having any impact on the active service of the employee concerned unless advised otherwise. Repeated absences of this kind shall constitute an offense and may result in disciplinary action and even removal from service.

## K. Festival Holidays
Employees of Code 19 may be entitled to avail the following festival holidays: International Mother Language Day (February 21) 1 day; Birthday of Father of the Nation (March 01) 1 day; Independence & National Day (March 26) 1 day; Bangali New Year (April 14) 1 day; International Labor Day (May 01) 1 day; Buddha Purnima* 1 day; Shab-E-Barat** 1 day; Shab-E-Qadr** 1 day; Eid-Ul-Fitr** 3 days; National Mourning Day (August 15) 1 day; Janmashtami* 1 day; Eid-Ul-Azha* 3 days; Ashura* 1 day; Durga Puja* 1 day; Eid-E-Miladunnabi** 1 day; Victory Day (December 16) 1 day; Christmas (December 25) 1 day. (* As per Government declaration. ** Depends on appearance of the Moon.)

## L. General Guidelines
(1) Employees cannot claim leave as right. The Management will have the prerogative to decline, curtail and/or ask the employee to resume duty before expiry of leave period other than sick leave and maternity leave.
(2) The employee must make reasonable effort to handover her/his responsibility to a fellow team member(s) during their leave of absence.
